Conversion Therapy

August Blue, Henry Scott Tuke, 1893–94

A pen, a secret, and a very understanding sibling.

“ooh, nice pen.”

“yeah. Hey! don’t unscrew it.”

“okay.? I was just curious what was rattling inside.”

“cyanide.”

“…why?”

“in case mum finds out.”

“yeah, that tracks.”
