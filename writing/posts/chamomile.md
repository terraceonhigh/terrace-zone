Who narrates the narrators?

The coffee pot runs dry after lunch and the herbal tea does not come on before 3pm, and strictly after she is done narrating the crime serial on page 4 destined for a zine in the 60s.

Which means she gets to boil the kettle, and wait.

And get to choose lemon ginger. Extra honey.

Something smoother, unlike the acrid whiteout she could smell on the other side of the manuscript; Something more unidirectional, than the new typewriter this writer got that can *backspace*.

Come to think of it, she is probably the one who makes the 3pm pot of tea

The narrator a booth over prefers Earl Grey, hot, but he gets to narrate Wattpad material and he can get his own tea if he wants it so bad.

She closed the studio door behind her at 5pm on Friday.
